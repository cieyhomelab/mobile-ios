Privacy Policy — Voice Calendar Assistant

Effective date: August 25, 2026

Voice Calendar Assistant ("the app") lets you create, view, and delete Google Calendar events using your voice. This policy explains what data the app handles, who it's shared with, and why.

What the app collects

  • Voice recordings. Audio captured while you hold the record button, used only to transcribe your spoken request.
  • Calendar data. Event details (titles, times, and attendees you speak) read from or written to your Google Calendar.
  • API keys you enter. Your own Anthropic and ElevenLabs API keys, entered on the Settings screen (this app is "bring your own key" — see below).

Third parties the app talks to

The app has no backend server. Each request goes directly from your device to the relevant provider below, using your own account/keys:

  • Google — Sign-In and the Google Calendar API, to read, create, and delete events on your calendar.
  • ElevenLabs — receives your voice recording to convert it to text (speech-to-text) and to generate the app's spoken responses (text-to-speech).
  • Anthropic — receives the transcribed text of your request to interpret it into a calendar action (create, read, or delete an event).

Each provider handles data it receives under its own privacy policy: Google, ElevenLabs, Anthropic.

Your API keys

Your Anthropic and ElevenLabs API keys are stored only in your device's iOS Keychain (via expo-secure-store). They never transit anywhere except directly to that key's own provider when the app makes a request on your behalf. The app itself never sees, logs, or stores your keys anywhere else, and they never leave your device except in a request to their own provider.

What we (the developer) retain

Nothing. The app has no server and no database — we do not collect, store, or have access to your voice recordings, transcripts, calendar data, or API keys. All data flows directly between your device and the third-party providers listed above.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new effective date.
